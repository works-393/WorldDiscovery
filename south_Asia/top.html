The Curator's Spotlight

The Taj Mahal, India:
The Jewel of Indo-Islamic Art

Commissioned in 1632 by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan to house the tomb of his favorite wife, Mumtaz Mahal, this ivory-white marble mausoleum remains the zenith of architectural craftsmanship in the subcontinent. It is a symphony of symmetry, geometry, and intricate pietra dura inlay.
